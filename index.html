06 / METHOD
Working method

A simple process from need to tested solution.

01

Needs analysis

Audience, context, objectives, constraints and expected outcomes.

02

Design

Learning pathway, sequence, formats and assessment logic.

03

Production

Activities, materials, digital resources and trainer support tools.

04

Testing

Check clarity, usability, timing and technical reliability.

05

Adaptation

Adjust content and delivery using participant evidence and feedback.

06

Evaluation

Measure outcomes and identify what should be retained or revised.

The method can be applied to a single workshop, a language course, a trainer-development programme or the design of digital learning resources.
